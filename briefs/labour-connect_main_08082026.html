JQZ is putting on a barbecue for the crew on their Parramatta high-rise site. Around 250 people will be there, about 40 of them Labour Connect workers. Other suppliers have the drinks, Labour Connect owns the barbecue, catered by Now We Meet. We are sending one creator for three hours to come away with 8 UGC videos: the hype cut, the setup, a worker POV, the queue at 12:30, two worker vox pops and two interviews.
