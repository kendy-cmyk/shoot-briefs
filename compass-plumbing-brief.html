ClientCompass Plumbing
IndustryTrades. Residential plumbing across sinks and blockages, drains, hot water, bathrooms and pipe re-lining.
LocationMichael's house is the main location. A second house is a 5-minute drive away. One team stop at a parking lot near Sydney Olympic Park.

Two things that shape the shoot
Residential only. No factory and no commercial jobs. Every frame should read as a normal suburban home.
Michael is not doing a talking head. He speaks off bullet points, no script and no teleprompter, and only appears at the open and close of the About Us. The answers get cut as voiceover.
